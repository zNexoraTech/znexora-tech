# Inbox: upload your weekly articles here

Put each Word (.docx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) file in the folder for its section:

- `inbox/theory/`     theory articles
- `inbox/practical/`  practical guides
- `inbox/news/`       news articles
- `inbox/weekly-recap/` weekly recaps

Give the Word and PowerPoint files the **same name** to combine them into one article.
A robot converts them within a few minutes and sends you a draft to approve.
