---
title: Privacy
---

ZNexora Tech does not use tracking cookies, analytics or advertising.

Your light or dark theme choice is saved only in your own browser.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

If you subscribe to our email updates, your address is handled by our newsletter provider under its own privacy policy, and you can unsubscribe from any email.
