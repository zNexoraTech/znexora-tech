ZNexora Tech represents innovation, cybersecurity excellence, digital transformation, and advanced technology solutions designed to help organizations navigate the future securely.

## Our mission

Technology moves fast, and so do the threats that come with it. We help organizations keep pace by turning the flood of security information into clear, trustworthy guidance they can act on.

## What we publish

**Latest news.** Short briefs on important security stories, written in our own words and always linking to the original report.

**Weekly recap.** Every Monday: the week's top stories, newly exploited vulnerabilities, ransomware activity and web defacement reports, plus practical actions for the week ahead.

**Exploited vulnerabilities.** Briefings on vulnerabilities that CISA has confirmed are being exploited in real attacks.

**Theory and practical guides.** Clear explanations of security concepts, and hands-on guides for defending systems.

## How we work

We combine automation with human judgment. Automation collects public information quickly; an editor reviews and approves every post before it is published. Ransomware figures come from criminal leak sites and are often unverified, so we report trends and never name the organizations listed.
